# TASLY HEALTHY TREATS — Design Brief

## Design read
A welcoming healthy-treats café website for people discovering the brand online; it should feel fresh, generous, tactile and easy to navigate, with food photography doing the emotional work rather than unsupported health claims.

## Concept spine
**The Fresh Table** — a continuous camera journey moves from natural ingredients into a beautifully composed table, while the site unfolds the café's identity, menu discovery, gallery and contact path.

## Delivery tier
Cinema. The scroll-scrub film is the primary hero interaction; supporting pages use restrained transitions and clear responsive layouts.

## Locked palette
- Deep leaf: #214A37
- Garden green: #47765A
- Cream: #F3F0E5
- Clean white: #FFFFFF
- Ink: #17251D
- Single accent: #B7C96A
The green is the dominant brand signal, cream and white create breathing room, and the muted pear accent adds a single fresh highlight without neon effects.

## Locked type
Cabinet Grotesk for expressive headings with Inter Tight for readable body copy. Both are sans-serif; the pairing gives the café a distinctive editorial voice without making it feel formal.

## Animation mode
animated-website — user selected Animated.

## Journey shape
single-shot — one café brand story told as a continuous closer look at a fresh table; a single clip avoids expensive, visually risky scene seams.

## Journey
1. The first impression — A composed table of fresh, colorful treats emerges from a calm, dark leafy setting. Headline: “A fresher kind of treat.”
2. The details — The camera glides closer across natural textures and carefully arranged food. Headline: “Made to feel good.” This is sensory copy, not a medical or nutrition claim.
3. The table — The frame opens to a generous shared spread. Headline: “Find your next favourite.”
4. The invitation — The camera settles on the table as the final beauty state. Headline: “Come discover Tasly.”

## World grammar
One continuous, photoreal food-editorial film. Overhead-to-low three-quarter camera move with a slow, steady push-in and subtle orbit. Deep leaf-green shadow environment, soft natural window light from upper left, matte ceramic and linen surfaces, crisp fresh produce textures, restrained cream highlights. Keep the focal food arrangement centered with clear negative space for HTML copy. No visible text, lettering, logos, watermarks, people, or brand-specific dishes.

## Mobile framing
Keep the central food arrangement inside the middle 60% of the frame. Use the mobile encode at up to 720px high, with chapter text on solid cream/green panels for legibility. Reduced-motion mode shows the exact poster and full static chapter content without fetching video.

## Site architecture
- / Home: animated brand introduction, clear menu and contact paths, editorial sections and footer.
- /menu Menu: searchable and filterable catalogue shell; no menu items, prices, ingredients or dietary labels will be invented. Show a clear editable-content state until Tasly supplies its current menu.
- /about About: brand introduction without invented founding date, founder biography, certifications or health promises.
- /gallery Gallery: generated café/food atmosphere imagery, accessible lightbox, keyboard close and focus handling.
- /contact Contact & Order: validated enquiry form with honest submission status; WhatsApp message builder that activates once an official number is configured; link to the supplied Instagram profile.

## Brand constraints
Use the supplied green/cream/white/earthy direction and the supplied Instagram profile as the brand reference. No official logo, fonts, menu data, phone number, address, hours, prices, email, ingredients, certifications or testimonials were supplied. Create a provisional text/monogram treatment that can be replaced; visibly identify missing business details in the editable content configuration, not as public fake facts.

## Section plan
Home: image-first cinematic hero; asymmetrical story chapter; editorial image strip; spacious menu invitation; contact banner. Distinct layout families, no repeated equal-card trio.
Menu: compact title lead; category/search controls; editorial empty state; ordering guidance.
About: large typographic introduction; offset image-and-copy block; values presented as neutral design principles rather than unsupported claims.
Gallery: full-bleed image-led mosaic; accessible lightbox.
Contact: contact introduction; structured form; social link and editable contact details.

## Asset plan
- One six-frame storyboard image for the single-shot journey.
- One 15-second scroll-scrub motion clip derived from the generated café still with a restrained continuous camera push-in, plus its exact first-frame poster. Higgsfield video generation was gated by the current account tier, so no paid video generation was submitted.
- Bespoke logo/monogram and favicon artwork.
- Three supporting food/café photographs for the About and Gallery pages.
- One branded 3:2 launch cover / OG image.
- A small consistent line-icon family for navigation and utility actions.

## CTA inventory
- Home hero primary: “Explore the menu” — underlined editorial link.
- Home hero secondary: “Contact Tasly” — framed text link.
- Menu product action: “Order on WhatsApp” — only active after official number and menu data are configured.
- Menu page CTA: “Ask about the menu” — contact route.
- Gallery lightbox controls: “Previous image”, “Next image”, “Close gallery”.
- Contact form: “Send enquiry” — validates fields and clearly reports that delivery is not connected until official contact details are supplied.
- Shared navigation CTA: “Contact”.

## Functional and content guardrails
Responsive at mobile, tablet and desktop. Semantic routes, accessible keyboard navigation, focus states, form labels and errors, gallery lightbox, search/filter state, metadata and social preview. No fake payment, login, booking, backend submission, fabricated contact details, made-up prices or menu claims. No runtime AI generation or Higgsfield integration in the visitor-facing product.
